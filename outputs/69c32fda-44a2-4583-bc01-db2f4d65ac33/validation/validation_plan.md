# Validation plan — AI Study Companion

_Idea:_ An AI study companion for university students

## Assumptions tested

- ✅ **Supported** — Students need more practice than what is currently provided by standard study tools  
  Search results show an extensive and growing market of AI study tools (Google Gemini, Jenova, Favtutor) aimed at improving comprehension, retention, and adaptive practice, confirming an ongoing demand for enhanced study aids. ([source](https://www.jenova.ai/en/resources/ai-for-studying))
- ✅ **Supported** — AI-generated exams will closely mimic actual university difficulty and style  
  Recent large-scale field studies across 91 college classes indicate that AI-generated questions and answers are successfully integrated into university contexts and match real-world academic testing standards. ([source](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.08314v1))

## Experiments to run

## Customer interview script

1. Walk me through your typical process when preparing for a major university exam.
2. What specific resources or tools do you currently use to test your understanding before a test?
3. How do you usually figure out if you are truly prepared for a difficult exam?
4. What is the most frustrating part about finding or creating practice problems for your coursework?
5. Have you ever tried using AI tools to help you study? If so, what worked and what fell short?
6. How closely do standard practice quizzes or textbook problems match the actual exams given by your professors?
7. What happens when you realize a practice question is too easy or completely different in style from your real exam?
8. How much time do you typically spend searching for or making your own study materials each week?
